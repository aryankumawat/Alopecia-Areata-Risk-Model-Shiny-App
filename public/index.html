GSE68801 Dataset Overview

This dataset includes gene expression profiles from scalp skin punch biopsies of patients with Alopecia Areata (AA) and healthy controls.

General Information

  • Platform: Affymetrix Human Genome U133 Plus 2.0 Array (GPL570)
  • Organism: Homo sapiens (human)
  • Total Samples: 122
  • Sample Type: Scalp skin punch biopsies

What is Alopecia Areata?

Alopecia Areata is an autoimmune condition in which the immune system mistakenly attacks the hair follicles, leading to hair loss on the scalp and elsewhere on the body.

Key Characteristics

  • Sudden onset: Hair loss can occur rapidly, often in round or oval patches.
  • Unpredictable: Hair may regrow and then fall out again.
  • Non-contagious: It is not caused by infections.

Types of Alopecia Areata

  • Patchy Alopecia Areata: The most common form, with isolated patches of hair loss.
  • Alopecia Totalis: Complete loss of scalp hair.
  • Alopecia Universalis: Complete loss of hair on the scalp and body.
